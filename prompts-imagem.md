## Direção comum a todos

Baseada na resposta real do Saulo (Q10): **escuro e sofisticado** + **técnico / profissional** — não "underground", não "vibrante/criativo".

Fotografia editorial documental. Luz prática quente contra fundo escuro dominante — mais clube de jazz refinado do que garagem. Paleta: preto e carvão profundo como base, madeira escura fosca, metal escovado, âmbar quente só como acento pontual (nunca dominante), toques de creme. Superfícies limpas e organizadas — o "técnico/profissional" significa precisão, não bagunça de equipamento. Textura, grão de filme sutil, imperfeição honesta — não polido feito showroom. Gente brasileira real.

**Proibido:** neon roxo/azul, LED colorido, glow, máquina de fumaça, flare, saturação exagerada, render 3D, sorriso de banco de imagem, estética "site gerado por IA", ambiente rústico/caipira (madeira envelhecida em excesso, jeito de garagem de fundo de quintal).

## Pacote — organizado pela ordem real da landing (D-020: Aulas/Ensaios lideram)

### 01 — Hero (control room / sala principal)

```
Editorial architectural photograph of a professional music recording studio
control room in São Paulo, Brazil. Wide angle, shot on 35mm film, dominant dark
background with warm practical lamps as the only light source. Large mixing
console in the foreground with matte black surfaces and brushed metal knobs,
acoustic wood diffuser panels on the back wall, a live room visible through
thick glass, softly out of focus. Sophisticated and technical mood — precise,
clean, professional, not rustic or cluttered. Deep charcoal and black palette
with warm amber accent light only, touches of cream. No neon, no purple or blue
LED glow, no lens flare. Slight film grain, honest shadows, dust in the light
beams. Empty of people. Horizontal composition with clear negative space on the
left third for text overlay. Photorealistic, high detail, editorial quality.
```

**Uso:** hero da landing e da comparativa. **Testando:** paleta escura/sofisticada e espaço negativo para o texto.

### 02 — Aula individual (violão/guitarra, adolescente)

```
Documentary-style photograph of a music teacher in his 30s guiding a Brazilian
teenage student playing acoustic or electric guitar in a small, sophisticated
teaching room. Both seated, focused on the instrument, teacher's hand pointing
at the fretboard without touching the student. Dark background, single warm
practical light from above creating a focused pool of light, rest of the room
in soft shadow. Matte black or dark wood furniture, acoustic panels visible but
unobtrusive. Candid mid-lesson moment, not posed for camera. Shot on 35mm film,
grain, shallow depth of field, 50mm lens. No neon, no colored light, no stock-
photo smile. Photorealistic, editorial documentary quality.
```

**Uso:** seção Aulas (líder da landing conforme D-020). **Testando:** se a cena de aula sustenta a paleta escura sem parecer sombria/triste.

### 03 — Aula em grupo / bateria (adulto iniciante)

```
Documentary-style photograph of an adult beginner student in his 20s or 30s
learning drums, mid-lesson, teacher standing beside the kit adjusting the
student's grip on a drumstick. Dark, sophisticated rehearsal room, warm
practical lighting from the side, drum kit slightly worn but well-maintained.
Motion blur on one drumstick to suggest movement. Focused, concentrated
expressions, not smiling at camera. Deep charcoal and black palette, warm amber
accent only. Shot on 35mm film, grain, honest imperfect framing. No neon, no
purple or blue glow. Photorealistic, editorial documentary quality.
```

**Uso:** seção Aulas — segunda imagem, mostra instrumento diferente (bateria) e público adulto (Q3: adolescentes + adultos iniciantes).

### 04 — Ensaio / sala coletiva

```
Documentary photograph of a Brazilian band rehearsing in an acoustically
treated rehearsal room, four musicians mid-song — electric guitar, bass,
drums, keyboard. Motion blur on the drummer's sticks, everyone focused on each
other rather than the camera. Dark background with a single warm practical
light source from above, wood floor, cables neatly routed along the wall,
gear cases stacked but organized, not cluttered. Deep charcoal and black
palette, warm amber accent, touches of cream. Real Brazilian people of mixed
ages and appearances, natural clothing, no branded logos. Shot on 35mm film,
grain, honest imperfect framing. No neon, no purple or blue glow, no smoke
machine. Photorealistic, editorial documentary quality.
```

### 08 — Detalhe técnico (equipamento)


## Curadoria obrigatória

- Mão com número errado de dedos
- Rosto deformado ou olhar "vazio" de IA
